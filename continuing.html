Continuing the Conversation with the Body

Optional practices and resources for ongoing integration

After the Session

After a powerful experience, different bodies ask for different things.

Some people notice energy that still wants to move.

Some notice a desire for rest.

Some feel creative, open or expansive.

Some feel tender, emotional or tired.

There is no right way to integrate. The invitation is not to force anything. The invitation is to listen.

If any of the resources below feel supportive, you are welcome to explore them. If not, that is completely okay too.

If you're noticing energy or activation

Sometimes the body continues processing through movement

You may notice energy, restlessness, a desire to stretch, walk, shake, move or simply change position more frequently. These are natural signs that the body is still in conversation with itself.

Gentle shaking practice Free movement to music Walking in nature Stretching
If you're looking for grounding and regulation

Sometimes the body asks for steadiness rather than intensity

Grounding practices can help us reconnect with ourselves, our surroundings and the present moment — gently and without demand.

Coherent breathing Barefoot walking Sensory grounding
If you're noticing joy, creativity or expansion

Not all integration involves release or difficulty

Sometimes we encounter joy, possibility, curiosity or a renewed sense of aliveness. These states are equally valid — and equally worth listening to.

Breath of Joy Dance or movement Creative expression Journalling
If you're feeling tender, emotional or tired

Sometimes the most supportive response is gentleness

Rest is a valid form of integration. There is no need to process, analyse or arrive anywhere. Simply being present with yourself is enough.

Yoga Nidra Gentle stretching Rest Connection with trusted people

Further Support

For those interested in exploring breathwork further — whether through individual sessions, group experiences or organisational work — details will be shared separately.

For now, there is nothing you need to do. Simply carry with you whatever felt useful, and leave the rest.

The door is simply here, if it ever feels worth opening.
